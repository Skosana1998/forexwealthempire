Refund & Cancellation Policy

Clear information about digital course purchases, payment issues, cancellations and student access at Liquidity Displacement University™.

Effective date: 24 October 2026
Please read before purchasing. This policy explains how refund and cancellation requests are handled. It does not remove consumer rights that apply under the law.

01About This Policy

This policy applies to educational products and services offered by Liquidity Displacement University™ ("LDU"), including online courses, recorded lessons, downloadable PDFs, mentorship and membership access.

02Digital Products and Course Access

LDU may provide digital learning materials through a student dashboard, including videos, PDFs, educational resources and online community access.

Once digital content has been delivered or access has been activated, a refund may depend on the circumstances, the purchase terms and applicable consumer protection laws. Digital delivery does not, by itself, cancel rights that the law gives a customer.

03How to Request a Refund

If you wish to request a refund, contact LDU through the official support channel published on the website. Please provide:

  • Your registered name and email address.
  • Your payment reference or transaction ID.
  • The course or service purchased.
  • A short explanation of your request.

Each request will be reviewed individually. LDU will communicate its decision and any next steps within a reasonable period.

04Monthly Payment Plans

If a programme is purchased through a monthly payment plan, the payment schedule, duration and cancellation conditions should be presented before checkout.

Cancelling a payment plan does not necessarily remove amounts already lawfully due. Any cancellation fees, future payment obligations or refunds must be consistent with the purchase agreement and applicable law.

05Duplicate or Incorrect Payments

If you are charged twice, charged the wrong amount, or money is deducted without your order being confirmed, contact support with your transaction reference.

LDU will investigate verified payment errors and arrange an appropriate correction or refund where required.

06Paid but Cannot Access the Course

If you have paid but cannot access your student dashboard or course materials, contact support so the payment and account can be checked.

LDU will make reasonable efforts to restore the access purchased. If the problem cannot be resolved, any available remedies will be considered under the purchase terms and applicable law.

07Account Misuse and Termination

Unauthorised sharing of login credentials, distribution of protected course materials, fraud or other serious violations may lead to account restrictions under the programme terms.

Any action affecting a student's access or refund rights remains subject to applicable law.

08Consumer Protection Rights

Nothing in this policy is intended to exclude, restrict or waive any rights that cannot legally be excluded or waived. Where applicable, South African consumer protection legislation and other relevant laws will apply.

If a term in this policy conflicts with a mandatory legal requirement, the legal requirement takes precedence.

09Trading Risk Disclaimer

LDU provides trading education. Financial markets involve risk, and losses are possible. Course participation does not guarantee profits, trading success, funded accounts or employment.

Trading losses alone do not determine whether a customer is legally entitled to a refund for an educational service.

10Contact and Support

For refund requests, billing questions or access problems, use the official support contact published on the LDU website.

Please keep your payment confirmation and transaction reference available so your request can be reviewed efficiently.

Need help with a payment?

Contact LDU through the support channel listed on our official website.
