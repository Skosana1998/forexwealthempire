TRADING PERFORMANCE REVIEW

Submit Trading Results

Submit your results for review by the LDU team. Upload screenshots of completed trades, including wins and losses.

Submission requirements
• Report the three months before your course and the three months after it.
• Upload up to 50 screenshots of completed trades.
• Do not upload passwords, private keys, bank details or other sensitive information.
• Your submission remains pending until reviewed by the programme team.
JPG, PNG or WebP only. Maximum 5 MB per image. Secure Storage configuration is required.
