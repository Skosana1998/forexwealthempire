About the methodology

Understand the process. Study the displacement.

Liquidity Displacement Methodology™ is an educational trading framework created to help students study how price interacts with liquidity, how directional movement develops, and how market structure can be used to organise analysis.

Rather than treating every price movement as an opportunity, the learning process focuses on observation, context, confirmation, and disciplined decision-making.

Students are encouraged to develop a repeatable analysis routine, document their observations, understand invalidation, and recognise when a market does not present a clear setup.

  • 04.Build a structured process for analysing potential opportunities.

Inside the educational framework

Build your understanding step by step.

The methodology is organised around essential market-analysis concepts. The objective is to understand the reasoning behind an analysis rather than blindly copy a trade.

MODULE 01

Liquidity Concepts

Learn what traders mean by liquidity, how significant highs and lows are studied, and why liquidity context matters when analysing price behaviour.

MODULE 02

Price Displacement

Study strong directional movement, candle behaviour, momentum, and how displacement can contribute to a broader market narrative.

MODULE 03

Market Structure

Explore price swings, structural changes, continuation, and the importance of evaluating movement within its wider context.

MODULE 04

Confirmation & Patience

Understand why a market touching a level does not automatically confirm a trade and why waiting for defined conditions can matter.

MODULE 05

Trade Planning

Learn to consider entry conditions, invalidation, position sizing, and risk-to-reward before making a trading decision.

MODULE 06

Discipline & Review

Develop a routine for recording decisions, reviewing mistakes, managing emotions, and recognising when there is no valid opportunity.
