ABOUT THE METHODOLOGY

More Structure. Better Preparation.

Liquidity Displacement Methodology™ is an educational trading framework developed under Forex Wealth Empire. It is intended to help traders organise their understanding of price action and evaluate potential trading opportunities with a structured process.

Instead of treating every price movement as an opportunity, students are encouraged to study market context, recognise relevant price behaviour, and establish conditions that must be present before considering a trade.

The goal is to encourage more deliberate analysis, consistent preparation, and awareness of trading risk. The course does not promise profits or guarantee that a strategy will work in every market condition.

COURSE OVERVIEW

Build Your Market Understanding

The course is intended to organise key trading concepts into learning areas that support preparation, analysis, and review. Final lesson content and delivery details should be confirmed before the course is sold.

01

Market Structure

Study directional behaviour, important price levels, and how market context can influence a trading decision.

02

Liquidity Concepts

Understand liquidity as a market concept and learn to evaluate price behaviour around relevant highs and lows.

03

Displacement

Explore strong directional price movement and its possible significance within a broader market context.

04

Trade Confirmation

Develop rules for evaluating whether the conditions for a potential setup have been met.

05

Risk Management

Study position sizing, risk limits, invalidation levels, and the importance of protecting trading capital.

06

Trading Review

Learn to document decisions, evaluate execution, and identify areas for improvement through review.
