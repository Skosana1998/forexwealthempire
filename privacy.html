Privacy & Data Protection

Your Privacy.
Your Trust.

This policy explains how Liquidity Displacement University™ may collect, use, store and protect personal information when you visit our website, register as a student or purchase educational services.

Effective date: 24 October 2026
Important: This policy must reflect the services, payment providers, analytics and technologies actually used by LDU. Please review it before publishing.

01Who We Are

Liquidity Displacement University™ ("LDU", "we", "us" or "our") provides trading education and related digital learning services. This policy applies to our website, student registration, accounts, educational materials and related support services.

Before publication, the operator's full legal name and appropriate business contact details should be added here.

02Information We May Collect

Depending on how you use our services, information may include:

  • Account information: name, email address, username and authentication records.
  • Student information: course enrolment, membership status, progress and support requests.
  • Payment information: payment status, transaction references, amounts and payment confirmations.
  • Technical information: device and browser details, log data and security events.
  • Communications: messages you send to support or through university community features.

We aim to collect only information that is reasonably necessary for the relevant purpose. Do not submit passwords or sensitive financial details through public forms or support messages.

03How We Use Information

Personal information may be used to:

  • Create and administer student accounts.
  • Authenticate logins and protect accounts against misuse.
  • Verify payments and manage access to paid courses.
  • Provide videos, PDFs, learning resources and student support.
  • Respond to enquiries, billing issues and refund requests.
  • Maintain website security and investigate suspected fraud.
  • Meet applicable legal, accounting and regulatory obligations.

Where required by law, we will obtain consent or identify another lawful basis before processing personal information.

04Payments and Payment Providers

Payments may be processed through an external payment service provider, such as PayFast, if that provider is enabled for your transaction. The provider may collect and process information under its own privacy policy and payment terms.

LDU may receive transaction references, payment confirmations and related information needed to verify an order and manage course access. The information we receive depends on the payment integration.

We do not claim to store complete card details unless this is actually part of an implemented and appropriately secured payment arrangement. Please consult the payment provider's privacy notice for details about its processing.

05Student Accounts and Course Access

If you register for LDU, we use relevant account and enrolment information to manage your access to student features and course materials.

Keep your login credentials confidential. Do not share your password or allow others to use your account. Contact support if you believe your account has been accessed without permission.

We may use access and security records to investigate suspicious activity and help prevent unauthorised access to student accounts.

06Cookies and Similar Technologies

The website or its service providers may use cookies or similar technologies to support essential website functions, authentication, security, preferences or analytics, if those features are enabled.

Essential technologies may be necessary for services you request. Where required, we will seek consent before using non-essential cookies or analytics technologies.

You can manage cookies through your browser settings. Disabling certain cookies may affect login or other website functions.

07When We Share Information

We do not intend to sell personal information to third parties. We may share information where reasonably necessary with:

  • Hosting and website infrastructure providers.
  • Authentication and database providers.
  • Payment processors handling your transaction.
  • Communication or support services used by LDU.
  • Professional advisers or public authorities where legally required.

Service providers should receive only the information needed for their assigned purposes and should handle it in accordance with applicable privacy and security requirements.

08Data Storage and International Transfers

Information may be stored by LDU or its service providers in the countries where their systems operate. Depending on the providers configured for the website, data may be processed outside South Africa.

Where applicable, we will take steps required by law for cross-border transfers, including appropriate safeguards for personal information.

09How Long We Keep Information

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, including account administration, course delivery, dispute resolution, security and applicable legal recordkeeping requirements.

Retention periods depend on the type of information and the reason it must be kept. Information that is no longer needed should be securely deleted, destroyed or de-identified where appropriate and legally permitted.

10How We Protect Information

We aim to use appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect personal information against unauthorised access, loss, misuse, alteration or disclosure.

No website or online transmission can be guaranteed completely secure. Users should protect their credentials and report suspected account compromise promptly.

Security also depends on the actual configuration of our hosting, database, authentication and payment systems.

11Your Privacy Rights

Subject to applicable law, you may have the right to request access to your personal information, correct inaccurate information, object to certain processing, withdraw consent where processing relies on consent, or request deletion where legally permitted.

South African data subjects may have rights under the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA). You may also have the right to raise a complaint with the Information Regulator of South Africa.

We may need to verify your identity before processing a request. Some information may need to be retained where the law requires it.

12Children's Information

LDU is intended for people legally eligible to use its services. If a service involves a child or a person whose information receives special protection under applicable law, additional requirements may apply.

If you believe a child has provided personal information in circumstances that were not permitted, contact us so we can review the matter and take appropriate action.

13Third-Party Websites

Our website may link to third-party services, including payment providers and social platforms. Those services operate under their own terms and privacy policies.

LDU is not responsible for the privacy practices of independent third-party websites. Review their policies before sharing information with them.

14Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy to reflect changes in our services, technologies or legal obligations. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated when a revised version is published.

Where required by law, we will provide notice or obtain consent before implementing changes that require it.

15Contact and Privacy Enquiries

For privacy questions, access requests or correction requests, use the official contact details published on the LDU website.

Before publishing this policy, add a monitored privacy contact email and the details of the responsible legal entity or Information Officer, where applicable.

Your information matters.

For privacy questions or requests, contact us through the official support channel on our website.
